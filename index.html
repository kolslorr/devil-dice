DEVIL DICE 3D

Roll, Match, and Chain in Isometric 3D

High Score: 0

HOW TO PLAY

You control a small devil on a grid filled with 3D dice. Clear dice before the board fills up!

Core Rules

  • Match Dice: Connect a group of dice showing the same value on top. The number of dice in the group must be at least that value (e.g., at least 3 dice showing 3, or 5 dice showing 5).
  • Sinking: Matched dice begin slowly sinking into the floor. While they sink, they can still be used as a walkway.
  • Chaining: Roll another die of the same value next to a sinking group to add it to the chain, restarting the sinking timer and getting huge point multipliers!
  • The Power of 1s: A die showing 1 on top cannot match on its own. However, if you roll a 1 next to any sinking group, it will immediately match and sink with them!

Movement & Controls

  • On Top of Dice: Walk from die to die. Walk off the edge of a die to descend to the floor.
  • On the Floor: Walk against a die to push and slide it in that direction. Sliding does not change the top face value!
  • Climbing: Press the CLIMB button (or Spacebar) when facing a die to climb onto its top. Note: You can climb any die, but you cannot push a die if it's blocked.

Desktop Controls

Move/Roll: Arrow keys or WASD (diagonal alignment for isometric board)
Climb/Descend: Spacebar or Enter
