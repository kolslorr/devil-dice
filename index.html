HOW TO PLAY

Directly interact with the dice grid using gestures — no character, just you and the dice.

Core Rules

  • Match Dice: Connect a group of dice showing the same value on top. The number of dice in the group must be at least that value (e.g., at least 3 dice showing 3, or 5 dice showing 5).
  • Sinking: Matched dice begin slowly sinking into the floor. While they sink, they absorb matching dice rolled next to them.
  • Chaining: Roll another die of the same value next to a sinking group to add it to the chain, restarting the sinking timer and getting huge point multipliers!
  • The Power of 1s: A die showing 1 on top cannot match on its own. However, if you roll a 1 next to any sinking group, it is instantly absorbed!

Gestures

  • Quick Swipe: Flick a die to Roll it into an adjacent empty tile (changes top face).
  • Hold & Drag: Press and hold a die for a moment, then drag to Slide it (preserves top face, pushes other dice).

Desktop Controls

Click & Drag: Same as touch — click a die and release quickly to roll, or hold then drag to slide.
Arrow Keys: Arrow keys select a die and roll it in that direction.
P / Escape: Pause game.
